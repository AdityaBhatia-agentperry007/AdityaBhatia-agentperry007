## 🔬 Research

**IIT Kanpur — Cryptography & Privacy Lab** *(6-8 months, ongoing)*

Working under Asst. Prof. Adithya Vadapalli on garbled circuits, oblivious transfer protocols, multi-party computation, and network security. This is not a summer internship — this is sustained research at one of India's premier technical institutions, conducted while still in high school.

Key output: [NeuralBhasha / D2AR](https://github.com/AdityaBhatia-agentperry007/D2AR-diffusion-vs-ar-hindi-nlp) — the most comprehensive benchmark of diffusion vs autoregressive language models for Hindi NLP to date. 8 state-of-the-art models evaluated across 4 tasks using a rigorous 5-step pipeline. Co-authored with Pavitra Kushwaha.
